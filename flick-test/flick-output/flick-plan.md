## Format
9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps.

## Brand assets
None supplied. Text wordmark "FuturEdge Strategies" only. Gold on near-black.

## Creative direction
Test run. Confident, clean, fast. Gold and black. No stock footage, no icons that could belong to any brand.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — leads-slipping-away — 0:00-0:06
Transcript: Most small businesses lose leads they already paid for.
What's on screen: A column of lead cards drops in one by one, each with a green "NEW LEAD" tag. Then, one by one, the cards slide off to the right and fade, leaving an empty column.
Text on screen: "Most small businesses lose leads they already paid for."
Brand assets: none.
Sequential / interaction: Headline types in. Four lead cards drop in, then slide away in order.
Sound effect: Typing.mp3 as the headline types. Pop.mp3 on each card drop.
Audio-coupled idea: Each card lands on its pop.
Transition: Crossfade.

### Scene 2 — capture-reply-book — 0:06-0:14
Transcript: A fast follow up system fixes that. Capture. Reply. Book.
What's on screen: Three large step tiles stack vertically: Capture, Reply, Book. Each is joined to the next by a gold connector line that draws downward. A check mark pops on each tile as it completes.
Text on screen: "A fast follow up system fixes that." Then "Capture", "Reply", "Book".
Brand assets: none.
Sequential / interaction: Tiles appear in order with the spoken words. Connectors draw. Check marks pop.
Sound effect: Click.mp3 on each tile. Correct.mp3 on each check mark.
Audio-coupled idea: Tile appears on the word.
Transition: Slide up.

### Scene 3 — futuredge-builds-it — 0:14-0:20
Transcript: FuturEdge Strategies builds it for you.
What's on screen: The three tiles shrink into one gold wordmark block reading "FuturEdge Strategies". A short tagline line sits beneath it.
Text on screen: "FuturEdge Strategies" / "builds it for you."
Brand assets: none (text wordmark).
Sequential / interaction: Tiles merge, wordmark glows once, then holds for a full second.
Sound effect: Impact.mp3 on the merge.
Audio-coupled idea: Merge lands on the impact.
Transition: End, hold 1 second.
